About Age Calculator

The CalculatorHubs Age Calculator calculates the difference between a date of birth and a selected calculation date. The result is displayed in years, months and days.

This tool can be useful when you want to determine someone's age on a particular date or calculate the time between two dates without performing the calculation manually.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate the age.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. The calculator will display the difference in years, months and days.

How Does the Age Calculator Work?

The calculator compares the year, month and day of the two selected dates. It calculates the difference between them and adjusts the months and days when necessary.

For example, if the day of the calculation date is earlier than the day of birth, the calculator adjusts the day and month difference using the previous month. A similar adjustment is made when the month difference is negative.

Example

Suppose the date of birth is 15 January 2000 and the calculation date is 20 March 2026. The calculator compares these two dates and returns the corresponding age in years, months and days.

You can change the calculation date to determine the age on a different past or future date.

Benefits of Using an Age Calculator

  • Calculates the difference between two dates.
  • Displays the result in years, months and days.
  • Useful for checking age on a specific date.
  • Saves time compared with manual date calculations.
  • Can be used on desktop and mobile devices.

Important Note

The result depends on the dates entered into the calculator. Changing the calculation date can change the resulting age. This calculator is intended for general age and date calculations.
