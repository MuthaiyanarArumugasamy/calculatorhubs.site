About Unit Price Calculator

The CalculatorHubs Unit Price Calculator helps you compare the cost of two products based on their quantity. Instead of comparing only the package prices, you can compare the price per gram, kilogram, millilitre, litre or item.

This can be useful when shopping for groceries, household products, personal-care products and other packaged goods.

How to Use the Unit Price Calculator

  1. Enter the price of Product A.
  2. Enter the quantity and select its unit.
  3. Enter the price and quantity of Product B.
  4. Select the appropriate unit for Product B.
  5. Click Compare Unit Prices.
  6. The calculator will compare the normalized unit prices.

What Is Unit Price?

Unit price is the cost of a product for a standard quantity or single unit. It makes it easier to compare products that are sold in different package sizes.

For example, one product may cost ₹100 for 500 grams while another costs ₹180 for 1 kilogram. Comparing only the package price can be misleading. Unit price provides a fairer comparison.

Unit Price Formula

The basic formula is:

Unit Price = Product Price ÷ Product Quantity

For products measured in grams or millilitres, the calculator converts quantities to a common base before comparing them.

Example

Suppose Product A costs ₹100 for 500 grams.

Its price per kilogram is:

₹100 ÷ 500 × 1000 = ₹200 per kg.

Suppose Product B costs ₹180 for 1 kilogram.

Its price per kilogram is ₹180.

Therefore, Product B has the lower unit price and is cheaper per kilogram.

Why Compare Unit Prices?

  • Helps identify the better-value product.
  • Useful when package sizes are different.
  • Reduces confusion caused by different quantities.
  • Useful for grocery shopping.
  • Helps compare bulk and smaller packages.
  • Saves time during price comparisons.

Important Note

For the most accurate comparison, Product A and Product B should represent the same type of product and use compatible units. For example, compare grams with kilograms or millilitres with litres rather than comparing unrelated measurements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a unit price?

Unit price is the price of a product for one standard unit of measurement or one individual item.

Why is unit price useful?

Unit price makes it easier to compare products with different package sizes and determine which option provides better value.

Can I compare grams and kilograms?

Yes. The calculator converts the quantities to a common measurement before comparing their unit prices.

Can I compare millilitres and litres?

Yes. Millilitres and litres are automatically normalized for the comparison.

Can I compare products sold individually?

Yes. Select Item as the unit when comparing products sold by the number of items.
